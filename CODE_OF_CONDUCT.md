# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders commit to making participation in
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, lives, and workflows.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes: harassment or insulting comments, personal
attacks, trolling, sexualization, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the repository owner via GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team is obligated to
maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
Violators may be excluded from the community, permanently if warranted.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within community spaces and in public
spaces when an individual is representing the project.
